Refund Policy

Clear information about course payments, subscriptions, cancellations and refunds.

Last updated: August 2026

1. General Policy

GOH Bible Academy aims to provide clear information about course fees and payment conditions before enrollment.

Because our courses are digital educational services, access may begin shortly after a successful payment. Therefore, refunds are subject to the conditions described below.

2. Before Making a Payment

Students should review the course description, duration, curriculum, price, subscription period and completion requirements before making a payment.

If you have questions about a course, please contact us before purchasing.

3. Digital Course Access

Once payment has been successfully verified, access to the purchased course may be activated.

If a student has substantially accessed or used the digital course materials, a refund may not be available except where required by applicable law or where we determine that a refund is appropriate.

4. Subscription Cancellation

Where a course is offered as a subscription, students may request cancellation of future subscription renewal where such cancellation functionality is available.

Cancellation of a future renewal does not automatically refund the current paid subscription period.

The student may normally continue using the service until the end of the already-paid period, subject to the course terms.

5. Automatic Recurring Payments

If automatic recurring payments are offered for a particular service, the renewal terms will be shown before authorization.

If you cancel before the next scheduled renewal, future charges should not be made through the applicable recurring payment arrangement.

If a renewal payment has already been successfully processed, refund eligibility will depend on the circumstances and applicable refund rules.

6. Failed Payment

If a payment fails, is cancelled or is declined, no refund is normally required because the transaction was not completed.

Course access may remain inactive until a successful payment is received and verified.

7. Payment Deducted but Course Not Activated

If money has been deducted from your bank account or payment method but your course access has not been activated, please contact us before making another payment.

We may verify the transaction with the payment provider before activating access or processing any refund.

Please keep your payment reference or transaction ID. It may be required to locate and verify the transaction.

8. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make the same payment more than once, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verification, an eligible duplicate payment may be refunded through an appropriate payment method or payment provider process.

9. Incorrect Payment Amount

If you accidentally pay an incorrect amount, contact us with your transaction information.

We will review the transaction and determine the appropriate action based on the circumstances.

10. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, you should immediately contact your bank or payment provider and notify us.

We may investigate suspicious or disputed transactions before taking action.

11. Refund Requests

A refund request should include sufficient information for us to identify the enrollment and payment.

  • Student name
  • Registered account information
  • Course or subscription name
  • Payment date
  • Payment or transaction reference
  • Reason for the refund request

12. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed individually based on the circumstances of the transaction.

A refund may be considered in situations such as:

  • Verified duplicate payment.
  • Verified payment processing error.
  • Payment received but the purchased service cannot reasonably be provided.
  • Other circumstances where a refund is required by applicable law.

A refund may not be available where a student has already substantially used the digital educational service.

13. Course Cancellation by the Academy

If GOH Bible Academy permanently cancels a paid program before providing the purchased service, we will review affected payments and may provide an appropriate refund or alternative arrangement.

The specific remedy will depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

14. Technical Problems

Temporary technical problems do not automatically create a right to a refund.

Where reasonably possible, we will attempt to restore access or resolve the technical problem.

If a technical problem prevents delivery of a purchased service for a significant period and cannot reasonably be resolved, the situation may be reviewed for an appropriate remedy.

15. Examination & Certificate Fees

Where examination or certificate services form part of a course purchase, eligibility for a refund may depend on whether those services have already been used or completed.

Completion of an examination or issuance of a certificate may affect refund eligibility.

16. Approved Refunds

When a refund is approved, we will normally use the original payment method or another reasonable method agreed with the student where appropriate.

The actual time required for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank or card issuer.

A refund approved by GOH Bible Academy may take additional business days to appear in the student's account because third-party payment providers and banks may require processing time.

17. Payment Provider Processing

Payments may be processed through PayHere or another third-party payment provider.

Payment processing, reversals and refunds may therefore be subject to the payment provider's technical procedures and processing timelines.

18. No Cash Refund Promise

We do not guarantee that every payment can be refunded in cash or through a different payment method.

Where a refund is approved, the method may depend on the original transaction and payment provider requirements.

19. Misuse and Fraud

Refund requests connected to fraudulent activity, unauthorized account sharing, false payment information or deliberate misuse of the service may be rejected.

We reserve the right to investigate suspicious transactions.

20. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy when our services, payment methods or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the updated date.

21. Relationship With Our Terms

This Refund Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy .

If a mandatory legal requirement provides a greater right than this policy, that legal requirement will apply.

22. Contact for Refund Requests

For payment or refund questions, contact GOH Bible Academy through the official contact details published on our website.

Please include your payment reference so that we can identify the transaction efficiently.
