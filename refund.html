Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which a student may request a refund for a paid GOH Bible Academy course or service.

Important

Please review the course description, price, access period and requirements before completing your payment.

1. Eligibility for Refund

Refund requests may be considered where there is a genuine payment or technical problem that prevents the student from receiving the purchased service.

2. Course Access

Once course access has been successfully provided and the student has substantially used or accessed the paid course materials, the payment may become non-refundable.

3. Duplicate Payments

If a student is accidentally charged more than once for the same course enrollment, the duplicate payment may be reviewed for a refund.

4. Technical Problems

If a technical problem prevents access to a purchased course, students should contact us first so that we can investigate and, where possible, restore access.

5. Fraudulent Transactions

Transactions identified as fraudulent or unauthorized may be investigated before any refund is issued.

6. Refund Request

To request a refund, contact GOH Bible Academy with:

  • Student name
  • Registered email address
  • Payment date
  • Payment reference or transaction details
  • Reason for the refund request

7. Review Process

Each refund request will be reviewed individually. Additional information may be requested where necessary.

8. Processing Time

Where a refund is approved, the processing time may depend on the payment provider and the original payment method.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For refund questions or requests, please contact GOH Bible Academy through the contact information provided on our website.
