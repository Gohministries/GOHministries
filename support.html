Student Support

Need help with your studies, examinations, progress or certificate? We are here to assist you.

📚 Course Help

If you have questions about your monthly lessons, Bible studies or course progress, check your course page first.

How do I continue my studies? Open My Course and select the month you are currently studying.
My progress is not updating Refresh the page and make sure you are connected to the internet.
I cannot open a lesson Check your login session and try again. If the problem continues, contact support.

📝 Examination Help

Monthly examinations are part of the academic requirements for completing the GOH Bible Academy program.

Examination is locked Complete the required lessons before attempting the examination.
Exam result is missing Refresh your Progress page. If the result remains missing, contact support.
I need help with an exam Contact the Academy support team before attempting the exam again.

🎓 Certificate Help

Your certificate becomes available after all required academic and ministry requirements have been completed.

Certificate is locked Check your Certificate page to see which requirements are still pending.
MCQ requirement A minimum of 480 correct answers from 600 MCQs is required.
Examination requirement All 12 monthly examinations must be completed.

👤 Account Help

Manage your account through your Profile page.

Login problem Return to the login page and sign in again using your registered account details.
Account information Open Profile to view your registered student information.
Security Never share your password or authentication information with others.

Need Further Assistance?

If you cannot find an answer here, contact Gate of Heaven Ministries for assistance with your GOH Bible Academy account or studies.
