MONTH 01 • STUDY NOTES

New Life in Christ

Understanding salvation, repentance, faith and your new identity in Christ.

LESSON 01

Understanding Salvation

Salvation is God's gracious work of rescuing sinners from sin and bringing them into a restored relationship with Him through Jesus Christ.

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God.” — Ephesians 2:8

Salvation is not something we earn through good works. It is God's grace received through faith in Jesus Christ.

  • We are saved by God's grace.
  • Jesus Christ is the way of salvation.
  • Salvation includes forgiveness of sins.
  • The believer begins a new relationship with God.
Key truth: Salvation is God's gift through Jesus Christ, received by faith.
LESSON 02

Repentance

Repentance means turning away from sin and turning toward God. Genuine repentance involves a changed heart and a changed direction of life.

“Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.” — Acts 3:19
  • Recognize sin.
  • Confess sin before God.
  • Turn away from sinful ways.
  • Turn toward God and His Word.
  • Continue walking in obedience.
Key truth: Repentance is a genuine turning of the heart toward God.
LESSON 03

Faith in Jesus Christ

Biblical faith means trusting Jesus Christ, believing His Word and depending upon Him. Faith is more than simply knowing facts about Jesus.

“For we walk by faith, not by sight.” — 2 Corinthians 5:7
  • Believe in Jesus Christ.
  • Trust His finished work.
  • Depend on God's promises.
  • Live according to God's Word.
Key truth: Faith trusts God even when we cannot see the complete picture.
LESSON 04

A New Creation

When a person comes to Christ, God begins a new work in that person's life. The believer receives a new identity in Christ.

“Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.” — 2 Corinthians 5:17

The Christian is no longer defined by the old life. Christ gives a new purpose, new hope and a new direction.

Key truth: In Christ, the believer has a new identity and a new direction.
LESSON 05

Forgiveness and Assurance

God forgives those who come to Him through Jesus Christ. Christians can trust God's promises instead of living continually under condemnation.

“There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus.” — Romans 8:1
  • Confess your sins to God.
  • Trust God's forgiveness.
  • Do not return deliberately to your old life.
  • Walk daily in God's grace.
Key truth: The believer can trust God's forgiveness and promises.
LESSON 06

Walking with Christ

The Christian life is a daily walk with Jesus. Spiritual growth requires prayer, Bible study, obedience and fellowship with other believers.

  • Spend time with God in prayer.
  • Read and study the Bible.
  • Obey God's Word.
  • Participate in Christian fellowship.
  • Serve others.
“As ye have therefore received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk ye in him.” — Colossians 2:6
Key truth: Following Christ is a daily journey of faith and obedience.
LESSON 07

Freedom from the Old Life

In Christ, believers are called to leave behind sinful patterns and pursue a life that honors God. This involves continual spiritual growth.

  • Put away sinful habits.
  • Guard your thoughts.
  • Choose godly relationships.
  • Practice self-control.
  • Seek God's strength when tempted.
Key truth: New life in Christ should produce a transformed way of living.
LESSON 08

Identity in Christ

A Christian's identity is rooted in Christ rather than in past failures, achievements or the opinions of other people.

  • You belong to Christ.
  • You are called to live for God.
  • You have a new purpose.
  • You are called to reflect Christ's character.
“But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people.” — 1 Peter 2:9
Key truth: Our identity and purpose are found in Christ.
LESSON 09

Living for Christ

The new believer is called to live a life that represents Jesus Christ. Our words, actions, relationships and decisions should honor God.

“And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord.” — Colossians 3:23
  • Honor God in daily decisions.
  • Love other people.
  • Forgive others.
  • Serve faithfully.
  • Be a witness for Christ.
Key truth: The Christian life should demonstrate the character of Christ.
LESSON 10

Growing as a Disciple

Every believer is called to continue growing in Christ and helping others know Him. Discipleship is a lifelong journey.

“Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.” — Matthew 28:19
  • Continue learning God's Word.
  • Grow in prayer.
  • Serve the church.
  • Share the Gospel.
  • Help others follow Christ.
Key truth: A disciple follows Jesus and helps others become disciples.
MONTH 01 REVIEW

Before Taking the Examination

Review all ten lessons carefully before taking the Month 01 examination.

  • Understand salvation by grace through faith.
  • Understand biblical repentance.
  • Understand faith in Jesus Christ.
  • Know what it means to be a new creation.
  • Understand forgiveness and assurance.
  • Know the importance of prayer and Bible study.
  • Understand your new identity in Christ.
  • Learn how to live as a disciple of Jesus.
