Serving Christ and His People

Gate of Heaven Ministries exists to glorify God and share the message of Jesus Christ through worship, prayer, Bible teaching, evangelism and community outreach.

Our desire is to see people come to know Christ, grow in faith, discover God's purpose and become a blessing to their families and communities.
