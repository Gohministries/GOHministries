About Us

Who we are and what we believe

Gate of Heaven Ministries is committed to sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ and helping people grow in faith through the Word of God, prayer, worship and service.


Our desire is to see individuals, children and families experience the love of God, discover their purpose and become a blessing to their communities.

“Go into all the world and preach the gospel to all creation.”
