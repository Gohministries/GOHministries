About GOH Ministries

Serving God and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Gate of Heaven Ministries (GOH Ministries) is a Christian ministry dedicated to prayer, worship, Bible teaching, evangelism, spiritual growth and community outreach.

Our desire is to see people experience the love of God, receive salvation through Jesus Christ and grow as faithful followers of Christ.

Through church services, prayer meetings, evangelism, Christian media and community ministry, we seek to share the Gospel and bring hope to people.

  • Prayer and Intercession
  • Bible Teaching
  • Evangelism
  • Worship and Spiritual Growth
  • Community Outreach
  • Christian Media

Our Heart

We believe that Jesus Christ is the way, the truth and the life.

Our mission is to proclaim the Gospel and encourage people to live according to God's Word.

We desire to see lives transformed by the power and love of God.
