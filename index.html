ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Learn the Bible Systematically

GOH Bible Academy is an educational initiative of Gate of Heaven Ministries providing accessible Christian Biblical Studies resources for people who desire to learn God's Word.

The curriculum follows a structured learning pathway covering biblical foundations, Christian doctrine, spiritual life, church life, discipleship, evangelism, ministry and Christian leadership.

Lessons are designed for personal study, discipleship and continued spiritual development.

OUR STUDY APPROACH

Learn, Reflect and Apply

Biblical learning should influence both understanding and daily life.
