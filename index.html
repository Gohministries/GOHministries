Pass and Continue

Achieve at least 80% to unlock the next month's programme.

80% Pass Requirement

Students must achieve a minimum score of 80% in each monthly examination to progress to the next month of the programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the programme?

The programme is designed as a 12-month discipleship training journey.

How much does the programme cost?

The programme fee is LKR 1,500 per month.

What happens if I do not achieve 80%?

The student does not progress to the next month until the required examination requirement is successfully completed.

Can I study in my preferred language?

Yes. The programme is planned for English, Tamil and Sinhala learners.

Is this a university degree?

No. This is a Christian discipleship and Bible training programme offered by GOH Ministries. It should not be represented as a government or university-accredited academic degree unless separately accredited.

How do I start?

Create a student account, review the programme information and complete the enrollment process.
