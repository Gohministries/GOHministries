ABOUT THE ACADEMY

GOH Bible Academy

GOH Bible Academy is a Christian Biblical Studies learning platform operated by Gate of Heaven Ministries. The academy is designed to help students study the Bible and grow in their understanding of Christian faith through structured lessons and biblical teaching.

The academy provides a structured course consisting of 180 lessons and offers learning resources in English, Tamil and Sinhala. Students can access lessons, learn at their own pace and use the available support channels for questions about registration, accounts and course access.

Our goal is to make biblical learning accessible and encourage students to develop a deeper understanding of Scripture and Christian discipleship.

📖 180 Lessons Structured Biblical Studies course
🌐 3 Languages English, Tamil and Sinhala
🎓 Student Learning Study at your own pace
