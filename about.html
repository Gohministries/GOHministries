GATE OF HEAVEN MINISTRIES

About Our Ministry

Serving God, proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teaching His Word and encouraging people to walk in faith.

WHO WE ARE

Gate of Heaven Ministries

Gate of Heaven Ministries is a Christian ministry committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teaching the Word of God, encouraging believers, prayer and serving people with Christian love.

OUR FOUNDATION

Vision, Mission & Values

Our ministry seeks to honor God through faith, truth, love and service.

👁️

Our Vision

To see people know Jesus Christ, grow in God's Word and live a life of faith.

🎯

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, teach Scripture, pray for people and serve communities.

❤️

Our Values

Faith, biblical truth, prayer, love, integrity, compassion and faithful service.

WHAT WE DO

Our Ministry

Through our ministry activities and online resources, we seek to share the hope of Jesus Christ and encourage people in their relationship with God.

📖 Bible Teaching Teaching and studying God's Word to help believers grow in biblical understanding.
🙏 Prayer Encouraging people to seek God through prayer and dependence on Him.
📢 Gospel Sharing the good news of Jesus Christ and the message of salvation.
🤝 Service Serving people with compassion, practical help and Christian love.
OUR FAITH

What We Believe

Our ministry is centered on the Christian faith and the authority of God's Word.

✝️ Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, Savior and Lord.

📖 The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's Word and the foundation for Christian faith and life.

🙏 Prayer

We believe prayer is an essential part of a believer's relationship with God.

❤️ Christian Love

We believe followers of Christ are called to love God and love others.

🌍 The Gospel

We believe the Gospel of Jesus Christ is the good news of salvation and hope.

⛪ Christian Community

We believe believers are called to worship, fellowship, grow and serve together.
