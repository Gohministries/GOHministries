About GOH Ministries

Serving God and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Gate of Heaven Ministries (GOH Ministries) is a Christian ministry dedicated to prayer, worship, Bible teaching, evangelism, spiritual growth and community outreach.

Our desire is to see people experience the love of God, receive salvation through Jesus Christ and grow as faithful followers of Christ.

Through church services, prayer meetings, evangelism and community ministry, we seek to share the Gospel and bring hope to people.

  • Prayer and Intercession
  • Bible Teaching
  • Evangelism
  • Worship and Spiritual Growth
  • Community Outreach

Our Message

We preach Christ and Him crucified— the power of God unto salvation for all who believe.

Our mission is to proclaim the saving grace found through Jesus Christ and His finished work on the Cross.

We desire to see lives transformed by the power and love of God.

Our Vision

“Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.”
Isaiah 43:19 — KJV

Our vision is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, strengthen the Church, reach people with the message of salvation, encourage spiritual growth and prepare souls for the return of Jesus Christ.
