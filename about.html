GOH BIBLE ACADEMY

About Our Academy

GOH Bible Academy is a Christian Biblical Studies education initiative of Gate of Heaven Ministries, created to provide structured opportunities for people who desire to study the Bible and grow in their understanding of Christian faith and ministry.

Our Story

Biblical Learning With Purpose

Gate of Heaven Ministries has a passion for sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teaching the Word of God and serving people through Christian ministry.

GOH Bible Academy extends this vision through structured online Biblical Studies designed for students, believers and those who desire deeper knowledge of Scripture.

Our courses combine Scripture-based learning, structured lessons, assessments and examinations to provide a clear learning journey.

What We Focus On

  • Biblical knowledge and understanding
  • Christian doctrine and faith
  • Scripture-based learning
  • Christian character and practical living
  • Evangelism and ministry preparation
  • Continuous personal Bible study

About The Academy

What Students Can Expect

Structured Courses

Courses are organized into subjects and learning modules so students can progress systematically.

Online Access

Students can access their learning materials online and study according to the schedule of their program.

Assessments

Learning activities and assessments help students review and demonstrate their understanding.

Final Examination

Eligible students may complete a final examination according to the requirements of their selected program.

About Our Certificates

Certificates issued through GOH Bible Academy are ministry-issued certificates representing completion of the relevant academy program. They are not represented as government, university, UGC or other state-accredited academic qualifications unless such recognition is specifically stated for a particular program.
