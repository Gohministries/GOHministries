About GOH Bible Academy

Discipleship That Leads to Ministry

GOH Bible Academy is a Christian discipleship initiative of Gate of Heaven Ministries, created to help believers grow in God's Word, develop Christian maturity and prepare for faithful service.

Our Purpose

Why This Academy Exists

The academy is designed to move students beyond simply learning Bible facts toward a life of discipleship and service.

📖

Biblical Foundation

Build a strong understanding of Scripture and essential Christian teaching.

❤️

Spiritual Growth

Develop prayer, faith, obedience, character and a deeper relationship with Christ.

⛪

Ministry Preparation

Prepare believers to serve others through evangelism, discipleship and ministry.

Our Mission

Equip Believers to Serve

Our goal is to provide accessible Christian education that helps believers understand God's Word and apply it to everyday life.

Throughout the one-year journey, students are encouraged to grow spiritually and develop a heart for serving Christ and others.
