Privacy Policy

How GOH Ministries handles information, cookies and services.

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Introduction

GOH Ministries ("we", "us", or "our"), also known as Gate of Heaven Ministries, operates gohministries.org.

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining clearly how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name or email address when you contact us.
  • Information provided when using registration or educational services.
  • Messages or other information sent through our support channels.

We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage data.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and improve our website and services.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Provide lessons and educational resources.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Display and measure advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Advertising

GOH Ministries may use cookies and similar technologies to improve the website, understand visitor activity and support advertising.

Our website may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for advertising, communication, hosting, content delivery, payments, education or other technical functions.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. WhatsApp

If you contact GOH Ministries through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy and terms.

We use WhatsApp as one of our communication and support channels.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our website and services. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

10. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may contact us to ask questions about information associated with you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
