Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

GOH Ministries ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Gate of Heaven Ministries website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or use our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request prayer, submit an enquiry, make a donation, or communicate with the ministry.

Depending on the service you use, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, message and other information that you choose to provide.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Our website or third-party services may automatically collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited and general usage information.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, improve functionality, understand website usage and support advertising services.

5. Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services to display advertisements on our website. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements according to their own privacy policies.

If Google AdSense or another advertising network is used, visitors should review the applicable provider's privacy policy and advertising controls.

6. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and requests.
  • To provide ministry resources and services.
  • To respond to prayer requests when applicable.
  • To process and record donations when applicable.
  • To improve website performance and functionality.
  • To maintain website security.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to or use services operated by third parties. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes that are prohibited by applicable law.

10. External Links

Our website may provide links to other websites, social media platforms, Bible resources, payment services or ministry resources. Once you leave our website, the privacy policy of the external service applies.

11. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information when it is optional. You may also contact us regarding information you have previously submitted, subject to applicable legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by GOH Ministries, please contact us through our Contact page.
