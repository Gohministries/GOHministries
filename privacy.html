Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

GOH Bible Academy respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it when you use our website and learning services.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to GOH Bible Academy, operated by Gate of Heaven Ministries, and covers information submitted through our website, student registration, course enrollment, learning services and related communications.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Student account information
  • Course and learning progress
  • Assessment and examination results
  • Certificate and completion information
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Information you voluntarily send to us

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Provide access to enrolled courses.
  • Track course progress and assessments.
  • Process course enrollment and payments.
  • Provide certificates when requirements are met.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Improve our educational services.

4. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as PayHere. Payment processing may be subject to the payment provider's own privacy policy and terms.

We do not require your card or banking password to create a GOH Bible Academy account. Payment information should only be entered through the authorised payment method provided during enrollment.

5. Cookies and Technical Information

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, session information, or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences and provide essential website functionality.

We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and general usage information for security and website operation.

6. Student Information

Student information is used to provide educational services and maintain accurate academic records. Students should keep their login credentials secure and should not share their passwords with others.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell student personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the academy, process payments, provide technical services, maintain security or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain account, enrollment, academic, payment and certificate records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or comply with applicable requirements.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, payment services, social media platforms or other resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended to be used with appropriate parental or guardian involvement where required by applicable law. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

12. Your Choices

You may contact us regarding your personal information, account information or questions about how your data is used. Where applicable, you may request correction or removal of information, subject to legitimate record keeping and legal requirements.

13. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact GOH Bible Academy.
