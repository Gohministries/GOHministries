Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

GOH Ministries ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Gate of Heaven Ministries website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you visit or use our website and its related services.

2. Information We Collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request prayer, submit an enquiry, communicate with the ministry, participate in ministry activities, or make a donation.

Depending on the service you use, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, message, donation-related information and other information that you choose to provide.

3. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website, hosting provider, analytics services or other third-party services.

This information may include browser type, device type, operating system, approximate geographic location, referring website, pages visited, date and time of visits, and general website usage information.

This information is generally used to understand website usage, improve performance, maintain security and provide a better experience for visitors.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies. Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on your device that can help websites remember preferences, understand usage and provide certain functionality.

Cookies may be used for essential website functionality, security, analytics, preferences and advertising purposes.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

GOH Ministries may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

When advertising services such as Google AdSense are used, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to collect information about visits to this website and other websites. This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure and improve advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Where applicable, users may be shown personalized advertising based on information associated with their browsing activity. Users may manage or limit personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and through the controls provided by their browser or device.

For more information about how Google uses information when you use websites or applications that use its services, please review Google's privacy information and advertising controls.

6. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and messages.
  • To respond to prayer requests when applicable.
  • To provide ministry resources and services.
  • To communicate with individuals who contact the ministry.
  • To process and record donations when applicable.
  • To improve website content, performance and functionality.
  • To understand general website usage.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use or link to services operated by third parties, including hosting providers, analytics providers, advertising networks, social media platforms, communication services, payment providers and other ministry resources.

Third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. GOH Ministries does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, Bible resources, payment services, WhatsApp, YouTube and other ministry or informational resources.

When you leave our website and visit an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

9. Donations and Payments

If you make a donation through our website or through a third-party payment service, certain information may be processed by the relevant payment provider to complete and record the transaction.

GOH Ministries may receive limited information necessary to identify or record a donation, depending on the payment method used.

Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms, which apply to information processed directly by those providers.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information under our control from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or through our website.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information when providing it is optional.

You may also manage cookies through your browser or device settings and use available advertising controls provided by advertising providers such as Google.

Where applicable and subject to legal requirements, you may contact us to ask about personal information that you have provided to us.

13. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, protect our interests, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how information is handled, or your interaction with GOH Ministries, please contact us through our Contact page.

16. Acceptance of This Policy

By using the GOH Ministries website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required for specific data processing or advertising technologies, appropriate consent mechanisms may be provided.
