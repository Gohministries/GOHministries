Last updated: August 2026

GOH Bible Academy, operated by Gate of Heaven Ministries, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website and online learning services.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. GOH Bible Academy does not intentionally store complete card numbers, CVV numbers or other sensitive card authentication information on its own website.

If advertisements are displayed on our website, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for payment processing, analytics, advertising, hosting and other website functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users who are legally able to create an account and use online educational services. Parents or guardians should supervise minors who access online educational content.

9. Your Rights

You may contact us regarding your personal information, account information or privacy-related questions.

10. Changes to This Policy
